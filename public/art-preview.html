A kit that holds together.

Every wall orientation is rendered under the same light. The four bits are U=1, D=2, L=4, R=8. No finished wall sprite is rotated. Gate letters name the entry edge.

Stone with weight.

Short parapets and broad merlons keep the wall thickness visible. Four value steps replace texture, gloss and tiny masonry.

A room worth holding.
